Overview

Calico is a pet care booking platform that brings appointments, health records and reminders into one place for pet parents — and gives veterinary clinics, pet hospitals and grooming businesses the tools to fill their calendars and retain patients. Pet owners can find verified providers nearby, book vet visits and grooming, track their pet's health, and unlock assured discounts on every booking.

Marking Mindz partnered with Calico to build the product from the ground up: research and product design, the customer-facing mobile apps, the provider dashboard, and the backend that ties everything together — delivered as a single, cohesive platform.

The challenge

Booking pet care in India is still largely offline — a mix of phone calls, walk-ins and paper records. Pet parents struggle to find trusted clinics, remember vaccination dates and keep a single history for their pet. On the other side, clinics and groomers lose time to manual scheduling, no-shows and missed follow-ups.

Calico needed a platform that made booking effortless for pet parents while giving providers real operational value — not just a directory, but a two-sided system that both audiences would actually use every day.

Our approach

We treated Calico as one product with two experiences. Starting from user research, we mapped the pet-parent journey (discover → book → remember → return) and the provider journey (accept → manage queue → follow up → grow), then designed flows that keep both sides in sync in real time.

From there we shipped in weekly increments — a clean, mobile-first design system, native app experiences, and a provider dashboard — all backed by a scalable API. Every release was demoable, so Calico could validate with real clinics and pet owners as we went.
